About

A sound between
worlds

JOCHI is the musical project of Josefina, an Argentinian DJ and emerging producer based in Barcelona. Her sound moves between minimal / deep tech and underground tech house, driven by hypnotic grooves and subtle progression. With an engineering background, she blends structure with sensitivity — crafting music as a language where rhythm and emotion converge to create genuine connection.

She has brought her sets through the rooms of Macarena, Rouge Cocktail Club, City Hall, and Bridge 48 — where she is part of the Artist Growth program. Inspired by the digital culture and visual language of the late '90s and early 2000s, JOCHI weaves nostalgia, energy, and the pulse of new technologies into her artistic vision. Her work explores the dialogue between logic and intuition, intimacy and expansion — building a personal universe where her Argentinian roots and Barcelona present meet on the dancefloor.
